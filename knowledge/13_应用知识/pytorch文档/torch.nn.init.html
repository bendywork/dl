torch.nn.init · 权重初始化

Functions for initializing neural network weights. All operate in-place on the given tensor.

神经网络权重初始化函数。全部对给定 tensor 原地操作。

linear = nn.Linear(100, 50)
nn.init.xavier_uniform_(linear.weight)    # 推荐：tanh/sigmoid
nn.init.kaiming_uniform_(linear.weight)   # 推荐：ReLU 族
nn.init.normal_(linear.weight, 0, 0.02)   # GPT 式初始化
nn.init.constant_(linear.bias, 0)
